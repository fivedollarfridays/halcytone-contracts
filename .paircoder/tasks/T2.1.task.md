# Baseline + StreamBaseline pydantic models

Author the session-baseline contract. `StreamBaseline` captures one named stream's statistics over the 60s quiet-capture window (`mean`, `stddev ≥ 0`, `sample_count ≥ 1`). `Baseline` is the per-session envelope: a `dict[str, StreamBaseline]` keyed by reserved stream name from `RESERVED_STREAMS`, plus `duration_s` and `captured_at`. Dict keys are validated against the registry at parse time — unknown streams reject with a clear error so halcytone-core can't silently record a baseline for a nonexistent sensor.
